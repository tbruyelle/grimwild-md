Markdown syntax reference

Module files use standard markdown plus these custom fenced divs. The dice notation (xD, 1–8) inside headings is what tells the renderer what's a pool or challenge.

Pressure pool

One column per ## xD TITLE heading inside a .pressure-pool div.

::: {.pressure-pool}
## 4D Night Falls
- The sun sets
- Stars appear
:::

Pressure pool — repeat

The repeat property adds an icon indicating the pool restarts from 0 when filled.

::: {.pressure-pool repeat}
## 4D Night Falls
- The sun sets
- Stars appear
:::

Pressure pool — lock link

A >> TITLE line draws a lock link to another pool's heading.

::: {.pressure-pool}
## 4D Night Falls
- The sun sets
- Stars appear
>> Day Breaks
:::

::: {.pressure-pool}
## 4D Day Breaks
- The sun rises
- Birds sing
:::

Pressure pool — trigger link

A >>* TITLE line draws a trigger (circle-arrow) link instead of a lock. The target heading must exist in the same pool.

::: {.pressure-pool}
## 4D Night Falls
- The sun sets
- Stars appear
>>* Day Breaks
:::

::: {.pressure-pool}
## 4D Day Breaks
- The sun rises
- Birds sing
:::

Challenge

One card per ## xD | TITLE heading inside a .challenges div (the pipe may be omitted). Items starting with * are traits, - are moves, and x is the fail state.

::: {.challenges}
## 4D | Goblin Ambush
* sneaky
- Steal food
x You fall
:::

Challenge — lock link

A >> TITLE line inside a challenge draws a lock link to another challenge.

::: {.challenges}
## 4D | Goblin Ambush
* Sneaky
- Steal food
x You fall
>> Goblin Panic
## 2D | Goblin Panic
* Go crazy
- Move everywhere
:::

Linked Challenges with title

A > TITLE line draws a plain line (no icon).

::: {.challenges title="Negotiating Peace"}
## 4D | Duchess Lysandra
* lineal heir
- Show Strength
> Baron Reynard

## 4D | Baron Reynard
- Challenge Honor
:::

Useful pieces

Items the party can pick up. Renders as a right-pointing triangle list.

::: {.useful-pieces}
**Villagers**: Honest, hard-working people

- Refuse to speak on the problem.
- Disappear entirely after sundown.
- Friendly enough, otherwise.

**The Well**: Life in Dreghill seems to revolve around the small square
in the center of the village.

- Emits a peculiar *metallic* smell.
- Villagers insist on gathering there.
- Goblins are drawn to it.

**Mella Gortis**: The closest thing the village has to a leader

- Always gets in the PC's way.
- Curses in *goblintongue*.
- Has a soft spot for outsiders.
:::

Set it up

Prep steps before a session. Renders as a square checkbox list.

::: {.set-it-up}
**Goblin Plague**: Choose 2 causes of the sickness and 1 odd thing
that's ultimately a false trail.

- Villagers started eating exotic roots.
- A **Hag** (*pg. 106*) in the forest.
- Dead goblin in the well.

**Death**: Choose 1 usual outcome when a goblin dies and 1 rare
possibility that's always untimely.

- Dies like a goblin normally would.
- Reverts into a human, dead.
- Reverts into a human, alive.

**Desires**: Choose 2 goblins crave with wild abandon and 1 that
strikes them with irrational dread.

- *Shiny things*
- *Weapons*
- *Hijinx*
:::
